# Code of Conduct

## Our Commitment

PhotonIDE is an open-source project intended to support collaboration and the exchange of ideas.

Everyone participating in the project is expected to help maintain an environment that is respectful, constructive, and welcoming to people with different backgrounds, experiences, and levels of technical knowledge.

## Expected Behavior

Participants are expected to:

- Treat others with respect and courtesy.
- Communicate constructively and professionally.
- Accept reasonable criticism and differing opinions.
- Focus discussions on ideas, code, documentation, and technical decisions.
- Provide feedback that helps others improve.
- Respect the time, effort, and contributions of other participants.
- Respect the privacy of other community members.
- Follow the project's contribution guidelines.

## Unacceptable Behavior

The following behaviors are not acceptable:

- Harassment, intimidation, or personal attacks.
- Discriminatory or hateful language directed at individuals or groups.
- Threats, deliberate intimidation, or targeted abuse.
- Publishing someone's private information without permission.
- Deliberately disrupting discussions or collaboration.
- Other conduct that a reasonable person would consider inappropriate in a collaborative technical environment.

Constructive disagreement about code, architecture, or project decisions is acceptable. Personal attacks are not.

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies to interactions related to PhotonIDE, including repository issues, pull requests, discussions, and other spaces where participants represent or collaborate on the project.

## Reporting Issues

If you experience or witness behavior that violates this Code of Conduct, use the reporting or contact options currently available through the repository.

Please provide enough context to understand the situation, while avoiding the unnecessary disclosure of private information.

## Enforcement

Reports may be reviewed in light of the available information and context.

Depending on the circumstances, possible responses may include a request to stop the behavior, a warning, restrictions on participation, or removal of contributions or access where appropriate and available.

Actions should be proportionate to the situation. No particular response or resolution time is guaranteed.

## Acknowledgment

By participating in PhotonIDE, you are expected to follow this Code of Conduct.

Thank you for helping maintain a respectful and constructive open-source project.
